# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming and respectful community. Participants should
be considerate, communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, and
accept responsibility for mistakes and their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or degrading
language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention,
and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Reports can be sent privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially where practical.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing
the project in public. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation,
or take other action they consider appropriate to stop unacceptable behavior.
